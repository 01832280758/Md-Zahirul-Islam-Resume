How a dispatch day runs

  1. Plan

    Review the day's orders and loads, and match them to the vehicles available.

  2. Allocate

    Assign trucks and vendors, and confirm every vehicle will be there when needed.

  3. Dispatch

    Supervise loading and release vehicles so they reach their destinations on time.

  4. Track

    Follow fleet movement and fix shortages, vendor delays and route issues as they appear.

  5. Verify

    Check trip records, loading documents and vendor bills, then report in SAP and Excel.
